Privacy Policy

At SmartCalc Hub, we respect your privacy.

Our calculators are free to use. We do not ask users to create an account, and we do not collect personal information directly through calculator forms.

The values entered into calculators are processed in your browser and are not stored by us.

In the future, this website may use third-party services such as Google AdSense or analytics tools. These services may use cookies to show relevant ads or understand website traffic.

Users can disable cookies in their browser settings.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us through our Contact page.
